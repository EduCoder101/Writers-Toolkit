Tense Shifts for Flashbacks

Tense is the gear stick that moves between present and past.
Before
Simple past: "Her fingers gripped the edge."
Entering
Past perfect: "She had stood here before..."
Inside
Relax to simple past: "The crowd roared..."
Returning
Back to simple past: "The memory faded..."
Use past perfect ("had + verb") for the first sentence or two of the flashback. After that, relax into simple past.
