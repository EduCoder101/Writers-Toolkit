Step 1Where does your story happen?

Pick a place. Each colour is a different kind of story.

Which kind of story is it?

Step 2Who is your main character?

Give them a name, one strength and one worry. The worry matters: the best problems poke at it.

One strength

What are they good at?

One worry

What are they secretly scared of?

Step 3The pebble: what goes wrong first?

A small problem. Annoying, but fixable. Look for a pebble that pokes at worry.

Step 4The rock: what happens because of that?

A bigger problem that grows out of the pebble. Check it: is more worried now? Does "because of that" make sense?

Step 5The boulder: the biggest problem of all

Think first. A good boulder passes all three checks.

1
It is the hardest problem so far. It looks almost impossible.
2
It happens because of the rock.
3
Something cares about is at risk: safety, a friend, a dream or a promise.

Step 6Your sizzling start

Drop the reader straight into the pebble. No waking up, no weather, no "One day".

Step 7The climax: how does beat the boulder?

Your main character must be the hero of their own story. No dreams, no rescues, no "then we went home and had dinner".

Slow this moment right down when you write it: short sentences, the senses, a thought.

Step 8How does your story end?

Show what has learned or how they have changed.

Step 9Your story plan

In your book

Write your title. Copy your plan as dot points, one for each step. Then start writing your story with your sizzling start.
