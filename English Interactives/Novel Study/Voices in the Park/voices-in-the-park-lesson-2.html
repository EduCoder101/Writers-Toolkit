Today’s tool

How a sentence starts shows what matters.

The start of a sentence is its theme. A character puts first whatever they care about most.

At exactly 9:00 am, the hall was still noisy.

The smell of salt and vinegar drifted along the row.

Same hall. Who is speaking each time? How do you know?
