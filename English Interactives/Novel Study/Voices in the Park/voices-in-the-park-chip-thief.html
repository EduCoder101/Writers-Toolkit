The Chip Thief

The event: a seagull steals the hot chips at the beach. How do you know who is speaking?
Name
Voice 1Dad

Fourteen dollars fifty. That is what I paid for one large box of chips. I had given clear instructions: lid on, eyes up, nobody feeds the birds. For ten seconds I looked away to find the sunscreen. Ten seconds! When I turned back, the box was upside down in the sand and a seagull was strutting off with my lunch. Next time, we are bringing sandwiches.

Voice 2Max, aged five

It was the best thing I have ever seen in my whole life. The seagull came down like a rocket and it grabbed the biggest chip and it didn’t even say sorry and then all the other chips went flying up in the air like fireworks. Dad made a noise like a kettle. I named the seagull Kevin. Can we come back tomorrow and bring Kevin more chips?

Voice 3Zoe, aged thirteen

Everyone saw. The whole beach. Dad actually chased it, waving a towel and shouting about fourteen dollars. Max was cheering for the bird. I pulled my hat down and stared at my phone, but the girls from school were sitting right there on the next towel. So embarrassing. I am never eating chips in public again.

Voice 4The seagull

Golden. Salty. Unguarded. From high above the flags, I spotted the box and the big human looking the other way. Wings tucked, I dropped. One snap of the beak and the fattest chip was mine. Behind me, the humans squawked and flapped. Slow, noisy creatures. Tomorrow, I will bring my cousins.
