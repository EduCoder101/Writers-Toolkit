I do · First voice

Charles’ mother

Her font

Neat. Upright. Proper.

We went to the park today.

Word choice

What kinds of words does she choose?

Formal, proper words. She gives orders and judges people she has never met.

Sentence length

Are her sentences short or long?

Short, sharp commands. She gives her son orders, just as she would a dog.

Tone

What feeling sits underneath?

Cold, bossy and disapproving. Then panicky when Charles goes missing.

Typography

What does her font tell us?

A stiff, old-fashioned serif. Everything in its place, just like her.

Visual symbolism

What do the pictures add?

Autumn colours and falling leaves. Look for the shape of her hat hiding in the park.

Visual symbolism

Walking home

It is the same street. What has changed?

The street did not change. So what did?

Listening for: plain, everyday words and a tired, flat voice in a thick, heavy font. On the way there, winter greys, bare trees and gloomy streets. On the way home, the same street glows with light and colour, because Smudge has cheered him up. His feelings changed, so the picture changed.
