Step 3 · Draft

Write it four times.

The same event, told by four voices that sound nothing alike.

  • Word choiceDoes each character have their own words?
  • Sentence lengthShort and sharp, or long and flowing?
  • Sentence startsDoes each voice begin with what that character cares about?
  • ToneCan a reader hear the feeling underneath?

Step 4 · Read aloud to a partner

Glow and grow.

Glow

What worked well?

Write one thing you really liked.

Grow

What would make it stronger?

Write one suggestion.

Do the four voices sound truly different? Use the checklist to explain.

Step 5 · Refine

Make it better.

  1. Read your partner’s grow. Act on it.
  2. Swap three ordinary words for words only that character would use.
  3. Read it aloud once more. Does every sentence make sense?
